A real desktop experience — inside the browser

DexterOS behaves like an operating system for your SaaS stack, with an app launcher, windows, wallpapers and full admin control over what everyone can see.

🪟

Multi-window workspace

Open several tools at once in movable, resizable windows. Minimise, maximise, restore and close — the desktop stays right underneath, just like a PC.

🚀

Database-driven launcher

Every shortcut is a database row. Add an app in the admin console and it appears for your whole team instantly — no deployments, no front-end changes.

🖼️

Admin-curated wallpapers

Upload, edit, disable or delete wallpapers. Members pick from the active set and their choice is stored on their account and restored at every sign-in.

👥

Tenant & user management

Search, filter and manage members: change roles, suspend or reactivate accounts, reset passwords, review sessions and activity, all scoped to your workspace.

🛡️

Secure by default

PBKDF2-SHA256 passwords, opaque server-side session tokens, role-guarded APIs, rate limiting, strict CORS/CSP headers and validated input on every endpoint.

🧠

Graceful embed handling

Some sites forbid framing. DexterOS detects it, explains why and keeps the app reachable in a secure wrapper so nothing ever breaks.

Live in three steps

From zero to a managed team desktop in a couple of minutes.

1

Create your workspace

Register a workspace and you become its owner. Invite teammates with your workspace join code — every workspace is fully isolated from the others.

2

Add apps & wallpapers

Name, link, icon — that's all an app needs. Upload wallpapers, set a default and decide exactly what your members can see.

3

Your team signs in

Members get a personalised desktop: their own wallpaper, app grid, taskbar and Settings app for profile and preferences.

Admin console

Total control, none of the busywork

A separate, hardened console for workspace owners and administrators. Changes propagate to every member's desktop the moment you save them.

  • ✓App management — add, edit, delete, enable/disable, control visibility, set icons by emoji, upload or URL, pin to the taskbar.
  • ✓Wallpaper library — upload images or gradients, mark a workspace default, disable without deleting.
  • ✓User administration — search and filter members, inspect profiles, usage and sessions, suspend or remove accounts.
  • ✓Activity monitoring — an audit trail of apps, wallpapers, members and sign-ins across the workspace.
